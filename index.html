HOW IT WORKS

Simple, private, and local.

Harbour reads data/BMSData.accdb in your browser. The database file is served from this computer and is not uploaded to a remote service.

  1. Keep the database in the project’s data folder.
  2. Pick a table to preview its fields and records.
  3. Export the full table or choose specific fields. Downloads are CSV files that open in spreadsheet apps.

The Access reader library is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed to open the app. Database contents stay in your browser.
