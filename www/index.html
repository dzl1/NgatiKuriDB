Ngāti Kuri Iwi Register

The register is for the uri, the descendants, of Ngāti Kuri. Registering connects you with your iwi and lets the Trust Board keep you informed about hui, pānui and opportunities for registered members.

Register or sign in Who can register?

Who can register

You can register if you descend from Ngāti Kuri.

Ngāti Kuri whakapapa

Ngāti Kuri are descended from the original inhabitants, the founding peoples of the northernmost peninsula of Aotearoa, in Te Hiku o Te Ika. Ngāti Kuri also trace their whakapapa to the Kurahaupō waka, which first made landfall in the Ngāti Kuri rohe at Rangitahua, the Kermadec Islands.

If your whakapapa connects you to these tūpuna, you are welcome to register, wherever you live today.

What we will ask you

Your name, date of birth and contact details, and the names of your parents, grandparents and tūpuna through whom you descend from Ngāti Kuri. This helps us confirm your whakapapa and find you if you are already on the register.

Registering tamariki? Please contact us and we will help.

How it works

  1. Create your accountUse your email address. We send you a code to check it is yours.
  2. Tell us about yourselfYour details and your whakapapa to Ngāti Kuri.
  3. We check your detailsIf you are already on the register, we link your account to your record. If not, we add you.
  4. Keep them up to dateSign in any time to see your details and ask for changes. Our team checks every change before it is saved.

Your information is safe with us

Kept in Aotearoa

The register is stored in New Zealand. It is used only for the iwi register and to contact you about iwi matters, and is never sold or shared for marketing.

Seen only by authorised staff

Only authorised Trust Board staff can see the register, and every access is recorded. Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct your information at any time.
